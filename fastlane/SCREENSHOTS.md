## Demo media sources

The books selected for the demo are public-domain classics. Covers are from Standard Ebooks:

- [Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland — Lewis Carroll](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/lewis-carroll/alices-adventures-in-wonderland)
- [Pride and Prejudice — Jane Austen](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/jane-austen/pride-and-prejudice)
- [Frankenstein — Mary Shelley](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/mary-shelley/frankenstein)
- [The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes — Arthur Conan Doyle](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/arthur-conan-doyle/the-adventures-of-sherlock-holmes)

The offline playback demonstration uses “Down the Rabbit-Hole,” read by Kara Shallenberg, from [LibriVox’s Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland, version 2](https://librivox.org/alices-adventures-in-wonderland-by-lewis-carroll-4/). The audio and standalone cover downloads are not bundled in this repository or the production app.
